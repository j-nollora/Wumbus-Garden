Garden Mechanics & Rules

Energy & bucket: Tools cost 5 energy. Refill your bucket at the Well by selecting the Watering Can and clicking the Well (costs 10 energy).

Watering: Crops need 4–7 growth days depending on the crop and must be watered daily. Unwatered crops wilt overnight.

Fertilizer shield: Apply fertilizer to protect a crop from overnight disasters. It does not speed up growth or replace watering.

Selling crops: Sell harvested crops to Wumbus for coins, or give them to complete quests for EXP.
